EnglishJO Support

Help for students, teachers, and schools

Sign-in help

EnglishJO accounts are created by the student’s school or teacher. If a username, password, or account is not working, contact the administrator who provided the account.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice activities require Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions. Open iOS Settings, find EnglishJO, and allow both permissions. Use a quiet environment and speak after the listening indicator appears.

Learning content

Units, lessons, and vocabulary are assigned and managed by the school. Questions about available content should be sent to the student’s teacher.

Privacy

Read the EnglishJO Privacy Policy for details about account, learning, and voice information.
